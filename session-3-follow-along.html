2bInstall VS Code▾
  1. Go to code.visualstudio.com and click the big download button.
  2. Open your Downloads, double-click the zip, and drag Visual Studio Code into Applications. Open it from Applications. If your Mac asks "downloaded from the internet, are you sure?", click Open.

5

Your folders: Claude proposes, you trim

52–60 min

Four simple ideas decide where things go: business.md (the facts about you), skills/ (how you do each kind of job), scripts/ (steps that never change, session 5), and your tools (written in tools.md). Everything else is a room for one kind of work.

Propose my folders
Read business.md, needs.md and case-study.md. Propose a simple folder structure for my work:
- business.md, needs.md, case-study.md stay at the top
- skills/ for how I do each kind of job, one file each
- one numbered room folder per kind of work (01_, 02_...), each with a short CONTEXT.md
- a room for my case study task
- _inbox/ for new files, archive/ for old ones, tools.md listing the apps I use
Start small: only rooms my work needs now. Show me the plan as a tree and wait for my OK. Don't create anything until I say yes.

Example of what it might look like:

my-business/ business.md ← what you do, for whom, prices needs.md ← what you want help with case-study.md ← your one task tools.md ← apps you use, where exports land skills/ ← your session 2 skill goes here 01_clients/ 02_content/ ← e.g. your monthly check lives here _inbox/ archive/
Rename, merge or remove until it feels like your work. Only then say yes. A few clear rooms beat a big plan you'll never use.
