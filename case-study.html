Each session takes your case study one step further:

In each session, two people's case studies are worked on live, on screen. Everyone else does the same steps on their own task at the same time. You'll get your turn.

How to pick a good one

Good case studies

  • My monthly Instagram check
  • Welcoming each new client
  • My weekly class or newsletter email
  • Finding any document for a client in seconds
  • My monthly money overview

Too big or too vague

  • "Everything"
  • "Grow my business"
  • "Be more efficient"
  • "Sort out my whole computer"

Three quick tests. A good case study passes all three:

  • 1
    You do it at least once a month, so there's a lot to win.
  • 2
    You can bring real material for it: old examples, exports, notes (copies).
  • 3
    You can say what "done" looks like in one sentence.

Not sure? Pick the one that annoys you most from your task list. It's fine to switch next week if you find a better one.
